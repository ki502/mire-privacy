1. Service Summary

Mire helps users record their own face-care progress from photos. The app analyzes a selected or captured face photo on the device and shows face-care observations, routine suggestions, and change history.

Mire is not a medical device, diagnostic service, identity verification service, or attractiveness ranking service.

2. Data We Process

Mire may process the following data on the user's device:

  • Photos selected by the user or captured in the app
  • Face landmark positions detected from the photo
  • Analysis results such as face line, jaw line, cheek fullness, balance, skin tone, dark circles, forehead lines, eye fine lines, and smile lines
  • Routine check status and measurement history
  • Subscription entitlement status provided by the app store or RevenueCat

3. Local Processing

Face photos and analysis results are processed on the user's device. The app does not upload selected photos or face analysis results to a Mire server by default.

The current version does not send the user's face photo to a generative AI service.

4. Purchases

Mire Pro purchases and subscription status are handled through the app store and RevenueCat. RevenueCat may receive purchase identifiers and entitlement status needed to manage access to Pro features.

Purchase cancellation and refunds are handled through Google Play or the App Store according to each store's policy.

5. Data Retention

Photos, measurement results, and routine records remain on the user's device until the user deletes them or uninstalls the app. Users can delete stored records from the Settings screen.

Free mode keeps only the latest measurement. Pro mode can keep measurement history for comparison.

6. Data Sharing

Mire does not sell face photos or analysis results. The app may rely on platform services for purchases and app distribution, including Google Play, Apple App Store, and RevenueCat.

7. Permissions

Mire may request:

  • Camera permission to capture a face photo
  • Photo/media permission to select a photo
  • Billing permission to provide Pro subscriptions
  • Internet/network access for purchase entitlement, restore, network status checks, and optional AI reference proxy communication

Permissions are used only for the app features selected by the user.

8. Children's Privacy

Mire is intended for users who can legally use app store subscription services in their region. The product should not be marketed as a service for children.

9. User Control

Users can:

  • Choose whether to capture or select a photo
  • Delete stored records from Settings
  • Restore purchases from Settings
  • Cancel subscriptions through the app store account settings
