1. Service

Mire helps users record face-care progress from photos. The app provides visual observations, routine suggestions, and change tracking based on selected or captured photos.

Mire is not a medical device, diagnostic service, identity verification service, or attractiveness ranking service.

2. User Responsibility

Users are responsible for choosing photos they have the right to use. Do not use Mire to analyze another person's face without permission.

Users should not rely on Mire for medical, dermatological, psychological, legal, employment, insurance, identity, or safety decisions.

3. Pro Subscription

Mire Pro may unlock measurement history, change comparison, 7-day mood tracking, routine continuity, and recommended guide previews.

Subscription products are purchased through the app store. Billing, renewal, cancellation, refunds, and trial eligibility follow the policies shown by Google Play or the relevant app store at purchase time.

  • Monthly product ID: mire_pro_monthly
  • Yearly product ID: mire_pro_yearly
